# RFC-CDP-048 — Learn Protocol

Author: Kevin “Andie” Williams  
Status: Draft v0.4  
Series: Constitutional Decision Plane (CDP)  
Date: July 24, 2026  
Updates: RFC-CDP-048 v0.3  
Depends On: RFC-CDP-023, RFC-CDP-033, RFC-CDP-047, RFC-CDP-074

## Abstract

Defines how recorded outcomes become precedent, policy revision, and governance improvement.

Draft v0.4 replaces the free-text-only `variance_summary` with a typed variance structure. The structure distinguishes changed evidence, changed policy, changed standing or authority, changed procedure or computational substrate, and legitimate authority-pluralism divergence. It preserves narrative explanation while preventing every changed outcome from being flattened into undifferentiated drift.

## 1. Purpose

The Learn Protocol answers:

- how recorded outcomes become precedent or policy revision;
- what learning artifacts are generated;
- who may ratify learned changes;
- what changed between expected and observed outcomes;
- whether a changed outcome reflects drift, principled development, error, repair, or legitimate divergence among authorities.

## 2. Authority

Actors invoking this protocol MUST possess `LEARN` authority, or learning MAY be generated by a service whose outputs require ratification.

Learning authority does not create authority to rewrite the historical record, silently change precedent, or collapse distinct sovereign or jurisdictional authorities into one rule.

## 3. Preconditions

- Decision SHOULD be `RECORDED`;
- outcome evidence SHOULD exist;
- supporting record references SHOULD be attached;
- the applicable Decision Lifecycle Envelope SHOULD be referenced when one exists;
- computational-context references SHOULD be attached when model, harness, tool, retrieval, or supplied-context conditions materially shaped the decision or outcome.

## 4. Learning Artifacts

A Learn act MAY produce:

- precedent entries;
- policy update proposals;
- risk rule adjustments;
- schema recommendations;
- training or operational guidance;
- execution safeguards;
- computational-context recommendations;
- replay or re-adjudication recommendations;
- repair recommendations.

## 5. State Transitions

- `RECORDED → LEARNED`

A transition to `LEARNED` means a learning artifact was produced and governed. It does not mean every recommendation was ratified or that a prior decision was invalidated.

## 6. Variance Classification

A Learn act that compares an expected outcome, prior outcome, precedent, replay, or comparable decision SHOULD classify material variance.

Variance classification exists to answer:

> What changed, and does that change legitimately explain the different result?

The base taxonomy is:

- `evidence` — facts, records, observations, provenance, or available evidence changed;
- `policy` — governing policy, interpretation, threshold, precedent, or applicable rule changed;
- `standing_authority` — participation rights, standing, recusal, delegation, decision authority, or binding authority changed;
- `procedural_computational` — lifecycle procedure or computational substrate changed, including model, harness, tool chain, retrieval configuration, supplied memory/context, or execution constraints;
- `authority_pluralism_exclusion` — the outcomes arose under distinct, concurrently valid authorities, jurisdictions, sovereignty claims, treaty relationships, or normative orders under RFC-CDP-074; the difference MUST NOT be classified as drift solely because the outcomes diverge;
- `unexplained` — a material difference remains after available evidence and governed references are reviewed;
- `none` — no material variance was identified.

Implementations MAY define narrower subtypes, including separate `model`, `harness`, `tool`, `retrieval`, or `context` subtypes, while preserving the base category.

Multiple categories MAY apply.

### 6.1 Authority Pluralism Exclusion

`authority_pluralism_exclusion` is not another kind of drift.

It records that comparison across authorities is informative but non-identity-preserving: different legitimate authorities may reach different outcomes without policy failure, model drift, procedural inconsistency, or arbitrary treatment within either authority.

A Learn act using this exclusion MUST identify the relevant authority or sovereignty references and SHOULD explain whether any conflict, coordination, comity, appeal, or repair process remains open.

The exclusion MUST NOT be used to hide arbitrary inconsistency within the same authority.

### 6.2 Model Drift and Model-Family Capture

Computational variance and authority capture are distinct.

- A changed model, harness, tool, retrieval configuration, or supplied context is a reproducibility and variance concern.
- A model-generated proposal evaluated only by the same model family may be an independence, standing, or authority-capture concern under RFC-CDP-033.

The same event may implicate both, but neither classification substitutes for the other.

## 7. Recommended Payload

```json
{
  "decision_envelope_ref": "string",
  "observed_outcome": "string",
  "expected_outcome": "string",
  "variance_summary": {
    "material_variance": true,
    "categories": [
      "evidence | policy | standing_authority | procedural_computational | authority_pluralism_exclusion | unexplained | none"
    ],
    "subtypes": [
      "model | harness | tool | retrieval | supplied_context | execution_constraint | other"
    ],
    "narrative": "string",
    "changed_ref_sets": {
      "evidence_refs": ["string"],
      "policy_refs": ["string"],
      "standing_authority_refs": ["string"],
      "procedure_refs": ["string"],
      "computational_context_refs": ["string"],
      "authority_pluralism_refs": ["string"]
    },
    "unexplained_questions": ["string"]
  },
  "lessons": ["string"],
  "recommended_changes": ["string"],
  "precedent_refs": ["string"],
  "ratification_status": "pending | approved | rejected",
  "metadata": {}
}
```

`variance_summary.narrative` preserves ordinary-language explanation.

The typed fields do not replace adjudication. They make the claimed source of variance visible, referenceable, testable, and contestable.

If `material_variance` is `false`, `categories` SHOULD contain only `none`.

If `categories` contains `unexplained`, `unexplained_questions` MUST be non-empty unless an implementation profile records an explicit exception.

If `categories` contains `authority_pluralism_exclusion`, `authority_pluralism_refs` MUST be non-empty.

When a computational subtype is present, `computational_context_refs` SHOULD be non-empty. Absence MUST NOT be represented as proof that the computational context was unchanged.

## 8. Replay and Re-adjudication

Learn MAY recommend replay, re-adjudication, or both.

- **Replay** asks what the original procedure produces under the original governed path and computational context, to the extent those conditions can be reconstructed.
- **Re-adjudication** asks what should be decided under the current governed path and computational context, with changed conditions made explicit.

Replay does not prove legitimacy or correctness.

Re-adjudication must not overwrite the historical record or conceal the source of a changed outcome.

Exact computational replay may be impossible when a model, external service, retrieval source, or tool version is no longer available. In that case, the Learn artifact SHOULD record the reconstruction limit rather than claiming equivalence.

## 9. Ratification and Precedent

A learning artifact does not become binding precedent, policy, or constitutional interpretation merely because it was generated.

Ratification MUST identify:

- the authority ratifying the change;
- the scope in which the change binds;
- the precedent or policy records superseded, distinguished, or left intact;
- any authority-pluralism boundary limiting generalization;
- unresolved dissent, uncertainty, appeal, or repair conditions.

A changed model output is evidence for review. It is not by itself principled precedent development.

## 10. Principle

Propose defines the world. Learn corrects the world.

A system that records but does not learn becomes bureaucracy.

A system that learns without preserving what changed turns drift into doctrine.
